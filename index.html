Sandsong: Habit Tracker · for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

Questions, problems or ideas: write to admin@modulsoftware.com. A person reads every message.

How it works

  • Every habit goes one of two ways: you grow it, or you let it go.
  • To vote for a habit you are growing, press and hold its ring. One vote a day for each habit.
  • A habit you are letting go gets its vote when you close the day. If it happened, choose Note it.
  • Votes only add up. One day never undoes the others.

Common questions

I changed phones. Are my votes gone?
No, if you restore the new phone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. You can also export everything from Settings › Your data.

How do I cancel Sandsong Plus?
In the app: Settings › Membership › Cancel membership. Or in the Settings app: your name › Subscriptions.

I bought Plus but it isn't open.
Settings › Membership › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

The watch doesn't show my plate.
Open Sandsong on the iPhone once; the plate travels to the watch from there.

Notifications don't arrive.
Check that Sandsong may send notifications in the Settings app › Notifications › Sandsong, then turn the two check-ins on again in the app.
