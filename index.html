A habit tracker drawn in sand.

Each habit is a note with its own figure. Keep it, and the plate sings and a pinch of sand settles on its figure. Votes only add up.

How it works

  • Every habit goes one of two ways: you grow it, or you let it go.
  • To vote for a habit you are growing, press and hold its ring. The plate sings, and a pinch of sand settles on the habit’s figure. One vote a day for each habit.
  • A habit you are letting go gets its vote when you close the day. If it happened, choose Note it.
  • Votes only add up. One day never undoes the others.
  • Each month keeps its own plate. Finished months are kept in the Record.

Free, and Plus

  • Free, from day one: three habits, and this month and last month of the Record.
  • Sandsong Plus: six habits, every past month of the Record, and widgets.
  • Plus comes three ways. Yearly, with one free week for new subscribers who have not had a Sandsong Plus trial. Monthly. Or for good: one payment, and nothing renews.
  • Prices are shown in the app and in the App Store, in your local currency, before you buy.

Your plate stays with you

Sandsong does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your own devices.

Common questions

Do my iPhone and iPad share a plate?

No. iPhone and iPad each keep their own plate: they do not sync with each other, because Sandsong has no cloud of its own. Your Apple Watch shares your iPhone’s plate. Plus opens on every device signed in with the same Apple Account.

What is a vote?

One day’s mark for one habit. A habit you are growing gets its vote when you press and hold its ring. A habit you are letting go gets its vote when you close the day without it. One vote a day for each habit, and votes only add up.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes: Settings › Sound in the app. Each habit has its own note, and the plate sings it when you vote. The sound plays alongside your music, and stays quiet when your iPhone or iPad is in Silent mode.

I changed phones. Are my votes gone?

No, if you restore the new phone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. You can also export everything from Settings › Your data.

How do I cancel Sandsong Plus?

In the app: Settings › Membership › Cancel membership. Or in the Settings app: your name › Subscriptions.

I bought Plus but it isn’t open.

Settings › Membership › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

The watch doesn’t show my plate.

Open Sandsong on the iPhone once; the plate travels to the watch from there.

Notifications don’t arrive.

Check that Sandsong may send notifications in the Settings app › Notifications › Sandsong, then turn the two check-ins on again in the app.
