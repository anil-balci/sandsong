Privacy Policy

Sandsong: Habit Tracker · Modul Software · Effective 20 November 2026

The short version: Sandsong does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your own devices.

What the app stores, and where

Your habits, your votes, the hours you set and your settings are saved on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app’s own storage. The app’s widgets and its Apple Watch app read the same information on your devices. We, Modul Software, never receive it.

  • Apple Watch. When you use the watch app, your iPhone and your watch exchange your habit records directly with each other, through Apple’s watch connection. Nothing passes through a server of ours; we have none.
  • Backups. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, your Sandsong records are included in that backup, under Apple’s terms and your own settings.
  • Export. You can export everything as a file from Settings. The file goes only where you choose to send it.
  • Delete everything. Settings has a button that erases all of your Sandsong records, on the phone and on the watch.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Notifications, only when you turn on the two daily check-ins, or when you start the yearly plan’s free week, so that the app can remind you two days before the free week ends. The app asks once. Every notification is scheduled on your device, and none makes a sound.
  • Face ID or passcode, only if you turn on the app lock. Apple handles the check; the app only learns whether it succeeded.

You can refuse either, or change your mind later in the iOS Settings app.

Motion

The app reads which way your device is held, to let the light on the plate follow its tilt. iOS does not ask for this. The reading is used in the moment and never stored.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether you have Sandsong Plus.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in your device’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash and usage reports. This is Apple’s service and your choice; Sandsong itself sends nothing.

Children

Sandsong collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever began to collect data, we would ask you first.
